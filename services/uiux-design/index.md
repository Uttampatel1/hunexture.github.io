## How we work

- **1. Research**: Understand users, competitors, and market landscape
- **2. Wireframing**: Create low-fidelity layouts and information architecture
- **3. Visual Design**: Develop high-fidelity mockups and interactive prototypes
- **4. Testing & Iteration**: Validate designs with users and refine based on feedback
