## Frequently asked questions

### Native or cross-platform - which is better?

Cross-platform (React Native/Flutter) is cost-effective for most apps. Native is best for complex, performance-critical applications.

### How long does app development take?

A typical MVP takes 3-4 months. Complex apps with advanced features may take 6-9 months.

### Do you handle app store submissions?

Yes, we manage the entire submission process including app store optimization and approval requirements.
