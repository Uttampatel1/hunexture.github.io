# AI-assisted SEO and Content Engine

> Topic research, briefs and a publishing workflow that keeps human editors in charge.

Canonical URL: https://hunexture.com/portfolio/ai-seo-content-engine/

This is an illustrative example project. Any metrics shown on the site are illustrative, not verified client results.

## Overview

A content system that clusters search demand, drafts structured briefs, helps writers produce first drafts and tracks which pages earn rankings and leads.

## Challenge

Publishing was slow and inconsistent, and the team could not tell which articles actually produced leads.

## Solution

We built keyword clustering, brief templates and an editorial workflow with fact-check steps, plus a dashboard joining search data to form fills.
