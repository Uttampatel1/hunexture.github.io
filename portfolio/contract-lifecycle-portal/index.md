# Contract Lifecycle Portal

> Request, review, sign and track contracts with clause search and renewal alerts.

Canonical URL: https://hunexture.com/portfolio/contract-lifecycle-portal/

This is an illustrative example project. Any metrics shown on the site are illustrative, not verified client results.

## Overview

A portal where business teams request contracts from templates, legal reviews them with clause comparison, and everyone can find signed agreements and upcoming renewals.

## Challenge

Contracts lived in email threads and shared drives; renewals were noticed after the notice window had passed.

## Solution

We built template-driven requests, a review workflow with redline comparison, e-signature integration and a searchable repository with renewal reminders and role-based access.
