## 7. Review strategy

Rules:
- Ask only real clients who actually worked with us.
- Never write, buy or trade for reviews. Never ask staff or friends to post as clients.
- Do not offer money, discounts or gifts in return for a review. Clutch, Google and GoodFirms prohibit paid or incentivised reviews.
- Do not "gate" reviews, which means asking only happy clients or steering unhappy ones elsewhere. Ask everyone.
- Do not ask for a specific star rating or specific wording.
- Reply to every review, good or bad, politely and with facts. Do not argue in public.
- Do not publish quotes from clients without written permission. Our site currently shows illustrative outcomes only, not real testimonials, and says so.

How:
1. Ask after delivery or a milestone, when the client can judge the work (a 1 to 2 week gap works well).
2. Send the request from the person the client knows, by email or WhatsApp.
3. For Google: use the review link from the profile dashboard ("Ask for reviews"). Clients need a Google account.
4. For Clutch: Clutch contacts the client directly to verify and interview. Give Clutch a contact person who has agreed to be called, and tell the client what to expect. [FILL IN: client contact details, only with their consent.]
5. Send one reminder at most.

Message template (email or WhatsApp):
```
Hi [Name],

Thank you for working with us on [project]. If you have a few minutes, we would be grateful for an honest review of your experience with Hunexture on [Google / Clutch]:

[review link]

Please write whatever you think is fair, good or bad. We read every review and use it to improve. Thank you for your time.

[Your name]
Hunexture | info@hunexture.com | +91 90672 62552
```

Reply template for a review:
```
Thank you, [Name]. We are glad the [project] worked out for you. / Thank you for the feedback, [Name]. We are sorry about [issue]. Please write to info@hunexture.com so we can look into it.
```
