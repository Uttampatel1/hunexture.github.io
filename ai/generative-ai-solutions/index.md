## What we offer

### Corporate Knowledge Copilots

Intelligent search systems generating grounded answers based purely on your intranet.

- **Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)**: Eliminating hallucinations by strictly sourcing internal docs.
- **Multi-format Understanding**: Reading PDFs, PowerPoints, and Confluence pages.
- **Role-based Access**: Ensuring junior staff cannot use AI to query executive-only files.

Technologies: LangChain, LlamaIndex, OpenAI

### Creative Media Generation

Generating bespoke imagery and audio for marketing at absolute scale.

- **Brand-aligned Image Generation**: Fine-tuning diffusion models on your exact brand guidelines and color palettes.
- **Personalized Ad Copy**: Spawning thousands of A/B testing variations instantly.
- **Synthetic Voiceovers**: Generating lifelike voice narration in 50+ languages.

Technologies: Stable Diffusion, Midjourney API, ElevenLabs

### Automated Coding & Data Structuring

Assisting developers and data analysts by writing boilerplate instantly.

- **SQL Generation**: Translating English questions into complex SQL graph queries.

Technologies: GitHub Copilot integration, StarCoder, Claude 3.5 Sonnet

## Delivery phases

- **Discover (1 week)**: Pick the use case, check data access and agree the pass mark.
- **Prove (2 to 4 weeks)**: Build a working pilot on your data and measure it against the pass mark.
- **Build (6 to 12 weeks)**: Production code, integrations, access control and evaluation suite.
- **Operate (Ongoing)**: Monitoring for quality, cost and drift, with regular reviews.

## Risks and controls

- **Hallucinated content**: Grounding in sources, citations and human approval for publication.
- **Prompt injection and data leaks**: Input filtering, scoped tools and permission checks.
- **Runaway cost**: Caching, smaller models where possible, budget alerts.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which model will you use?

The smallest one that meets your quality bar. We compare hosted and open-weight models on your test set and show the cost per task.

### Can we keep our data private?

Yes. We use enterprise endpoints with no training on your data, or models hosted inside your own cloud account.
