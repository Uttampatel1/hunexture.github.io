## Delivery phases

- **Discover (1 week)**: Pick the use case, check data access and agree the pass mark.
- **Prove (2 to 4 weeks)**: Build a working pilot on your data and measure it against the pass mark.
- **Build (6 to 12 weeks)**: Production code, integrations, access control and evaluation suite.
- **Operate (Ongoing)**: Monitoring for quality, cost and drift, with regular reviews.

## Risks and controls

- **Wrong or invented answers**: Answers cite sources; low-confidence cases go to a person.
- **Language gaps**: Per-language test sets, including Hindi and mixed-language input where needed.
- **Sensitive text in prompts**: Redaction and private endpoints.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can it understand Hindi, Gujarati or Hinglish?

Modern models handle many Indian languages, with varying quality. We test your languages on real examples before promising results.

### Will it replace our support team?

It handles common questions and drafts replies. People stay in the loop for complex or sensitive cases.
