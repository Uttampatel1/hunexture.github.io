## Overview

Unlocking Actionable Value from Raw Data Lakes. Hunexture’s Data Science & Analytics practice builds robust pipelines that aggregate fragmented data into singular sources of truth. From descriptive dashboards to predictive statistical modeling, we empower leaders with foresight.

## What we offer

### Modern Data Warehousing

Centralizing your enterprise data architecture for lightning-fast queries.

- **ELT/ETL Pipelines**: Reliable scheduled ingests from hundreds of third-party APIs.
- **Data Cleansing**: Automated deduplication and anomaly imputation.
- **Data Governance**: Establishing clear ownership and data dictionary catalogs.

Technologies: Snowflake, dbt, Airflow

### Advanced Predictive Modeling

Going beyond historical reports to predict what will happen next.

- **Demand Forecasting**: Anticipating supply chain constraints months in advance.
- **Customer Lifetime Value Evaluation**: Identifying high-value cohorts before they spend a dime.
- **Dynamic Pricing**: Adjusting markup continuously based on algorithmic demand cues.

Technologies: scikit-learn, Prophet, XGBoost

### Interactive Business Intelligence

Real-time visualizations that tell a compelling strategic story.

- **Executive Dashboards**: High-level KPIs served in an intuitive, beautiful interface.
- **Self-Serve Analytics**: Empowering non-technical staff to slice data freely.
- **Automated Alerting**: Push notifications when critical metrics breach thresholds.

Technologies: Tableau, Power BI, Looker

- Forecasting demand, churn or revenue

## Deliverables

- Data model and pipelines
- Dashboards with agreed definitions
- Forecast or segmentation models
- Metric dictionary

## Delivery phases

- **Audit (1 to 2 weeks)**: Map sources, quality and the decisions the data should support.
- **Model (3 to 6 weeks)**: Build the pipeline and first dashboards.
- **Predict (4 to 8 weeks)**: Add forecasts and test them against history.
- **Operate (Ongoing)**: Data quality checks and new questions.

## Risks and controls

- **Conflicting definitions**: A shared metric dictionary signed off by owners.
- **Bad source data**: Automated quality tests that alert before reports go out.
- **Dashboards nobody opens**: Built around real decisions and reviewed after launch.
