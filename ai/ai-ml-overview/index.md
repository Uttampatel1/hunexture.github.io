## Overview

Strategic Consulting and Roadmap Development for AI. Initiating an AI journey requires intense strategic alignment. Hunexture’s advisory services demystify the AI landscape, mapping out high-ROI use cases and ensuring your data estate is primed before engineering commences.

## What we offer

### AI Readiness Assessments

Evaluating your organization’s preparedness for machine learning integration.

- **Data Maturity Audit**: Analyzing the quality, accessibility, and volume of your historical data.
- **Infrastructure Review**: Assessing your current cloud topology against modern MLOps requirements.
- **Skill Gap Analysis**: Identifying internal training needs for your technical teams.

Technologies: Strategic Frameworks, Data Quality Matrix, Cloud Security Audits

### Use-Case Discovery & ROI Calculation

Finding the intersection of technical feasibility and maximum business impact.

- **Ideation Workshops**: Collaborative sessions with executives to surface operational pain points.
- **Feasibility Scoring**: Ranking ideas based on data availability and algorithmic complexity.
- **Financial Modeling**: Projecting cost savings or revenue generation against implementation expense.

Technologies: Design Thinking, Financial Forecasting, Risk Assessment

### Roadmap Drafting & Prototyping

Creating actionable blueprints transitioning from strategy to execution.

- **Phased Execution Plans**: Breaking multi-year transformations into 6-week deliverable sprints.
- **Technology Selection**: Unbiased recommendations on build vs. buy, cloud providers, and LLM vendors.
- **Rapid Proof-of-Concepts (PoCs)**: Validating the core assumption with a low-fidelity pilot in 14 days.

Technologies: Agile Methodology, Jira Alignment, Figma Wireframing

## Delivery phases

- **Interview (1 week)**: Talk to leaders and teams about goals and constraints.
- **Assess (1 to 2 weeks)**: Review data, tools and skills.
- **Prioritise (1 week)**: Score ideas by value, effort and risk.
- **Plan (1 week)**: Roadmap, budget ranges and first pilot brief.

## Risks and controls

- **Chasing hype**: Every idea is scored against a business metric.
- **Underestimating data work**: Data readiness is assessed before any estimate.
- **No owner**: Each roadmap item has a named business owner.
