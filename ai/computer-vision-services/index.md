## Overview

Empowering Machines to See, Analyze, and Act. Our Computer Vision division at Hunexture creates robust AI visual systems replacing tedious human physical inspection. From manufacturing quality control to retail footfall tracking, we build systems that process video feeds in real-time.

## What we offer

### Industrial Fault & Defect Detection

Automated assembly line inspections moving at microsecond speeds.

- **Micro-defect Identification**: Find scratches, dents, and misalignments invisible to the human eye.
- **High-speed Processing**: Inferencing at hundreds of frames per second.
- **Automated Rejection**: Direct integration with robotic arms to sort out defective items.

Technologies: YOLOv8, OpenCV, NVIDIA TensorRT

### Facial Recognition & Biometrics

Highly secure, ethically-implemented identity verification systems.

- **Access Control**: Contactless door entry for corporate and high-security zones.
- **Liveness Detection**: Preventing spoofing via masks, photos, or digital replays.
- **Privacy-first Archiving**: Processing on the edge to avoid storing PII centrally.

Technologies: FaceNet, Dlib, MediaPipe

### Retail & Spatial Analytics

Map physical store floors like an ecommerce website.

- **Heatmapping**: Visualize which aisles get the most foot traffic.
- **Demographic Estimation**: Anonymous age and gender modeling.
- **Queue Management**: Alerting staff when checkout lines exceed operational capacity.

Technologies: DeepSORT, PyTorch, AWS Panorama

## Delivery phases

- **Capture (1 to 2 weeks)**: Collect sample images in real conditions and define classes.
- **Prove (3 to 4 weeks)**: Train and test on held-out images.
- **Deploy (6 to 10 weeks)**: Integrate with cameras, lighting and your systems.
- **Operate (Ongoing)**: Track accuracy as conditions change.

## Risks and controls

- **Lighting and angle changes**: Training on varied real conditions and periodic retests.
- **Privacy of people in frame**: Blur or on-device processing; clear signage and retention limits.
- **Rare defects**: Targeted data collection and anomaly detection.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many images do we need?

Often a few hundred per class to start, more for rare or subtle defects. A pilot tells us what is enough.

### Can it run without internet?

Yes. Models can run on edge devices at the site and sync results when connected.
