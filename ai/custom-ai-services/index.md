## Delivery phases

- **Discover (1 week)**: Pick the use case, check data access and agree the pass mark.
- **Prove (2 to 4 weeks)**: Build a working pilot on your data and measure it against the pass mark.
- **Build (6 to 12 weeks)**: Production code, integrations, access control and evaluation suite.
- **Operate (Ongoing)**: Monitoring for quality, cost and drift, with regular reviews.

## Risks and controls

- **Poor or missing data**: Data audit in week one; we say early if the data cannot support the goal.
- **Model drift**: Monitoring and scheduled re-evaluation.
- **Unclear ownership**: A named business owner and review cadence.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do we know if our idea is feasible?

We test it on a sample of your data in a short pilot with a written pass mark. If it fails, you have spent weeks, not months.

### Do we need a lot of data?

Not always. Pretrained models and retrieval need far less than training from scratch. We tell you what the approach requires.
