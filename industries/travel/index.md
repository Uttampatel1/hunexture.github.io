## Frequently asked questions

### Do you integrate with airline and hotel booking systems?

Yes, we integrate with major GDS systems like Amadeus, Sabre, and Travelport, plus direct hotel chains, Booking.com, and activity booking APIs like Viator.

### How do you handle payment processing?

We support multiple payment gateways with PCI DSS compliance, supporting credit cards, digital wallets, BNPL, and local payment methods across 100+ countries.

### Can travelers modify or cancel bookings?

Yes, our self-service booking management module handles modifications, cancellations, and automated refund processing based on fare rules and supplier policies.

### Do you support package booking (flight + hotel + transfers)?

Yes, our dynamic packaging engine assembles custom travel packages from live inventory in real time, applying bundle discounts and presenting a single itinerary.

### How do you handle multi-city and open-jaw itineraries?

Our booking engine supports complex multi-city, open-jaw, and round-the-world itineraries with real-time fare combinations across multiple carriers and OTAs.

### Which travel suppliers can you connect?

Common GDS, hotel, car and activity APIs. Access depends on your supplier agreements, which we help you scope.

### Can you support multiple currencies and languages?

Yes, from the start. Pricing, taxes and content are structured so adding a market is configuration, not a rebuild.
