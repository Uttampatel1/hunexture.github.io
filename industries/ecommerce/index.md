## Frequently asked questions

### Can you integrate with existing inventory systems?

Yes, we provide seamless bi-directional integrations with ERP systems, dedicated WMS platforms, and fulfillment centers for real-time inventory synchronization.

### Do you support international selling?

Absolutely. Our platform supports multi-currency pricing, multi-language content, international shipping rate calculation, and localized tax compliance.

### How do you optimize conversion rates?

We implement A/B testing on key pages, personalized product recommendations, abandoned cart recovery flows, one-click checkout, and continuous heatmap analysis.

### Do you support subscription and recurring billing?

Yes, we build recurring billing with Stripe, Braintree, or Razorpay supporting weekly, monthly, and annual cycles with automatic retry logic and dunning management.

### How do you handle returns and refunds?

We build configurable returns management workflows, automated refund processing based on return reason, and reverse logistics integration with major carrier partners.

### Should we go headless?

It helps when you need custom experiences or many channels. For a simple catalog, a hosted platform is often faster and cheaper.

### Can you migrate from our current platform?

Yes. We plan product, customer and order migration with redirects so search rankings are protected.
