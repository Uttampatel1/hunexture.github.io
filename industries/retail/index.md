## Frequently asked questions

### Can you integrate with existing POS systems?

Yes, we provide integrations with major POS systems including Square, Lightspeed, and Shopify POS, plus custom integrations for proprietary retail systems.

### How do you handle inventory synchronization?

Our real-time inventory management system syncs across all channels within seconds, preventing overselling and automatically adjusting product availability.

### Do you support multiple payment methods?

Yes, we integrate with all major payment gateways and support credit cards, digital wallets, BNPL (Klarna, Afterpay), and local payment methods globally.

### Do you support headless commerce?

Yes, we build headless commerce architectures decoupling the frontend from the backend, giving you maximum flexibility for web, mobile, voice, and IoT shopping channels.

### Can you integrate with our existing ERP and WMS?

Absolutely. We provide bi-directional integrations with SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, and major WMS platforms for real-time inventory and order management synchronization.

### Can you connect our POS and online store?

Yes. We sync stock, prices and orders so both channels read from one source of truth.

### Do you build on Shopify or custom?

Either. Shopify is faster for standard catalogs; custom suits complex pricing, bundles or workflows. We recommend after discovery.
