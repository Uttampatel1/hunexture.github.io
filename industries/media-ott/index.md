## Frequently asked questions

### What video quality and formats do you support?

We support 4K, HDR10, Dolby Vision, and adaptive bitrate streaming in HLS, DASH, and Smooth Streaming formats across all devices.

### How do you handle content protection?

We implement industry-standard DRM solutions including Widevine, PlayReady, and FairPlay, plus forensic watermarking to protect premium content.

### Can the platform handle live events?

Yes, our platform supports ultra-low latency live streaming with WebRTC and LL-HLS for sports, concerts, and live events with millions of concurrent viewers.

### Do you support offline viewing and downloads?

Yes, we implement secure DRM-protected downloads so subscribers can watch content offline on iOS and Android apps within license expiry windows.

### How do you handle content licensing and geo-restrictions?

Our rights management module automatically enforces geographic restrictions, blackout windows, and license term rules based on viewer IP location in real time.

### Can you build live streaming as well as on-demand?

Yes. Live needs lower latency and different capacity planning, which we scope separately from on-demand video.

### Do you build smart TV apps?

We build for Android TV, Fire TV, Apple TV, Roku and Samsung or LG TVs, usually sharing a core with the mobile apps.
