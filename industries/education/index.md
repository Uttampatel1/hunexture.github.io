## Frequently asked questions

### Does the platform support both live and recorded classes?

Yes, our platform supports live virtual classrooms with recording capabilities, allowing students to review content at their own pace and catch up on missed sessions.

### Can parents track their child's progress?

Absolutely. We provide dedicated parent portals with real-time access to grades, assignments, attendance records, and teacher messages.

### Is the system mobile-friendly?

Yes, our platform is fully responsive and includes dedicated mobile apps for iOS and Android with offline learning support.

### Do you support SCORM and xAPI content?

Yes, our LMS is fully SCORM 1.2, SCORM 2004, and xAPI (Tin Can) compliant, importing and tracking e-learning content from any authoring tool including Articulate and Adobe Captivate.

### Can students learn offline?

Yes, our mobile app supports offline mode, allowing students to download lessons, complete assignments, and take quizzes without an internet connection.

### Can the platform work with an existing LMS?

Yes. We build to LTI and common LMS APIs so your tools plug into Moodle, Canvas or similar systems.

### How do you protect children's data?

We collect the minimum, separate identity from learning data, require parental consent where the law demands it, and set short retention periods.
