## Frequently asked questions

### Can you integrate with existing telecom infrastructure?

Yes, we have extensive experience integrating with OSS/BSS systems, NMS/EMS platforms, and legacy mediation systems from Amdocs, Ericsson, Nokia, and custom platforms.

### How do you ensure billing accuracy?

Our mediation system validates every CDR in real time, with reconciliation tools and automated discrepancy detection to ensure revenue assurance across all services.

### Do you support prepaid and postpaid models?

Yes, our convergent charging system supports prepaid (OCS), postpaid (offline charging), and hybrid models with configurable rating and flexible billing cycles.

### Do you support number portability and regulatory reporting?

Yes, we have built-in MNP management, TRAI/FCC regulatory reporting, lawful intercept interfaces, and QoS monitoring compliant with local telecom regulations.

### Can your solution handle 5G network slicing?

Absolutely. Our platform supports dynamic 5G network slice creation, lifecycle management, SLA monitoring, and per-slice billing for enterprise private network deployments.

### Can you work inside our existing toolchain?

Yes. We build on your monitoring, ticketing and CI tools instead of adding new ones.

### Do you build customer portals for telecom?

Yes: self-service, usage, billing and support, connected to your OSS and BSS systems.
