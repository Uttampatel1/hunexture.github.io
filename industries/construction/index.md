## Frequently asked questions

### Does the system work offline on construction sites?

Yes, our mobile app includes a robust offline mode with automatic sync when connection is restored — field data is never lost even in remote locations.

### Can you integrate with BIM software?

Absolutely. We provide integrations with Revit, AutoCAD, BIM 360, Navisworks, and Trimble for seamless design-to-field data flow.

### How do you track equipment and materials?

We use QR codes, RFID tags, and GPS tracking to monitor equipment location, utilization, and maintenance schedules in real time across all sites.

### Does the system handle subcontractor management?

Yes, subcontractors get their own portal to view assigned tasks, submit progress updates, upload documents, and communicate directly with the main contractor.

### How does weather impact tracking work?

We integrate with weather APIs to automatically log weather delays in project timelines, notify stakeholders, and generate updated completion date forecasts.

### Does it work with BIM tools?

We can link to common BIM exports and viewers. The depth of integration depends on your model standards.

### Will it work with poor site connectivity?

Yes. Apps store work locally and sync when a connection returns.
