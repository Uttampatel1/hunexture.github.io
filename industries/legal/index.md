## Frequently asked questions

### Is the system secure for confidential legal documents?

Yes, we implement bank-level encryption, role-based access control, and audit trails to ensure complete confidentiality and attorney-client privilege protection.

### Can it integrate with existing legal research databases?

Absolutely. We provide integrations with Westlaw, LexisNexis, Bloomberg Law, and PACER for seamless research within your workflow.

### How does the billing and time tracking work?

Our system offers automatic time capture, customizable billing rates by matter and timekeeper, LEDES invoicing, and integration with QuickBooks and Xero.

### Can the system migrate data from existing practice management tools?

Yes, we provide comprehensive data migration from Clio, MyCase, PracticePanther, and custom systems with complete data integrity verification and zero data loss.

### Do you offer AI-powered contract analysis?

Absolutely. Our AI reviews contracts, identifies risks, flags unusual clauses, highlights missing provisions, and compares against your standard templates automatically.

### Is it safe to use AI on privileged documents?

Only with the right setup: a private model endpoint with no training on your data, access limited by matter, and logging. We design for that and document the risk for your ethics counsel.

### Can you integrate with our practice management software?

Often through its API or export tools. We confirm the options during discovery before promising a scope.
