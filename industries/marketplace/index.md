## Frequently asked questions

### How do you handle vendor payments and commissions?

Our platform automates payment splits, commission calculations, and vendor payouts on configurable schedules with complete transparency and tax reporting.

### Can vendors manage their own stores?

Yes, vendors get dedicated dashboards to manage products, orders, inventory, promotions, and analytics — fully white-labeled to your brand.

### How do you prevent fraudulent sellers?

We implement multi-level identity verification, automated fraud scoring, review authenticity checks, and chargeback protection to maintain marketplace quality.

### How do you handle disputes between buyers and sellers?

Our structured dispute resolution workflow allows evidence submission from both parties, mediator assignment, and automated refund processing based on resolution outcome.

### Do you support international marketplace expansion?

Yes, we handle multi-currency pricing, local payment methods, cross-border tax compliance, logistics integrations, and localized seller dashboards for global markets.

### How do you solve the chicken-and-egg problem?

Software cannot do it alone. We help you launch narrow, in one category or city, and build the tools to onboard the scarcer side first.

### Which payment partners work for split payouts?

Stripe Connect, Razorpay Route and similar. The right one depends on your countries and model.
