## Frequently asked questions

### How do you handle content moderation?

We implement AI-powered moderation combining NLP and image recognition with human review queues, appeals workflows, and community reporting tools.

### Can the platform scale to millions of users?

Yes, our microservices architecture and auto-scaling cloud infrastructure can support millions of concurrent users across feeds, chat, and live streaming.

### Do you support real-time notifications?

Yes, we implement push notifications, in-app real-time alerts, and email digests with user-configurable frequency and notification type preferences.

### How do you ensure GDPR compliance for user data?

We build with privacy-by-design principles — data minimization, consent management, right-to-erasure workflows, and data portability APIs fully implemented from launch.

### Can users monetize their content on the platform?

Yes, we build creator economy features including subscriptions, content tipping, pay-per-view, brand deal matching, and transparent revenue sharing with payout dashboards.

### Can AI moderation replace human moderators?

Not safely. AI sorts and flags at scale; people decide edge cases and handle appeals.

### How do you handle media storage costs?

Transcoding profiles, tiered storage and CDN caching keep cost per user predictable.
